How to Request Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how users of the Lastory app (developer: Lastory Operating Team) can request deletion of their account and associated data. You can request deletion using the steps below even if you no longer have the app installed.

Steps to Request Account Deletion

Option 1. Delete directly in the app (processed immediately)

Open the Lastory app → go to your Profile/Settings screen → select Account → select Delete Account → confirm after verifying your identity as prompted.

Once confirmed, the deletion process for your account and data begins immediately.

Option 2. If you can't access the app (request by email)

Send an email with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your account to the address below. Including your account email or nickname in the message body helps us process your request faster.

Email: lastorylaststory@gmail.com

After verifying your identity, we process deletion requests within 5 business days and notify you by email once completed.

Data That Is Deleted

Once account deletion is complete, the following data linked to your account is deleted:

  • Account information (email address, Firebase Authentication user ID (UID), nickname/username, profile photo)
  • Memories and Records (text, photos, audio recordings, questions and answers, creation/modification dates, and related information)
  • Recipient information (name, relationship, phone number or email address)
  • In-app notification data
  • Other service data linked to that account

Data Retention

As a general rule, Lastory deletes all of the data listed above upon account withdrawal and does not separately retain personal information after deletion.

If applicable law requires certain information to be retained for a specific period, that information is kept only for the period required by that law and is not used for any other purpose. For more detail, see Articles 3 and 7 of the Privacy Policy.
